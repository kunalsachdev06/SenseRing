LET'S GET CONNECTED

Set up SenseRing

Use Chrome or Edge on a Windows 10 or 11 laptop (64-bit).

This browser cannot connect your ring. Open SenseRing in Chrome or Edge on your Windows laptop.

Checking your laptop

Looking for the SenseRing companion.

  1. 1

    Download your companion

    Everything you need is included. No separate software installation.

    Download for Windows ↓
  2. 2

    Extract and open

    Choose Extract all, keep the folder somewhere permanent, then open Start-SenseRing.cmd. Your companion runs quietly and opens the website.

  3. 3

    Connect your ring

    Keep your finger clear while the ring starts. On the dashboard, choose Connect ring and select SmartRing. Leave this page open, then switch to your music or presentation app.

Need a hand? ⌄

Connection is blocked

Check this site's Bluetooth and local-network permissions in your browser, then retry. If your laptop is managed, ask its administrator about blocked permissions.

Controls are not responding

Keep this page open and focus the app you want to control. If the companion is unavailable, retry below or close and reopen it from its folder.

Replacing an older companion

Open Stop-SenseRing.cmd in the previous folder before starting the new copy.

Start automatically at sign-in

Optionally open scripts → Install-Startup.cmd in your companion folder. Use Remove-Startup.cmd there to undo it. Otherwise open Start-SenseRing each time you use the ring.
